Calendar subscriptions

Outlook, Google Calendar, Apple Calendar and almost everything else can subscribe to a calendar link. Each lecturer gets their own under Your account. There is nothing to install and nothing to re-approve when a token expires, because there is no token to expire.

Webhooks

Every timetable change is posted to your URL as JSON, signed with the endpoint's own secret in an X-Chalkline-Signature header. This is how you connect anything not listed here, including Zapier, Power Automate and your own scripts.
